== 1. OBJECTIVE
Test whether the predictive meaning of sequences depends on context and location: P(outcome | sequence + context).

== 2. DATASET AND SPLIT

== 6. STATISTICAL METHOD
Effect = candidate expectancy (2R target, stop 1R) minus same-session, same-direction baseline.
t-statistic is the paired mean of per-trigger effects over its standard error. Wilson intervals for win rates; bootstrap (2000 resamples, seed 7) for expectancy.
